Your plants, watered on the day they need it.

Tell Sprout what is on your windowsill. It works out a rhythm for each pot from its size, the season and how bright the room is, and taps you on the shoulder when it is time.

Get Sprout

Three things it keeps track of, so you do not have to

  • How thirsty each pot is

    A fern in a small pot dries out in days. A snake plant in a deep one can go a month. Sprout keeps a separate clock for each.

  • How much light the room gets

    Plants drink faster in a bright south window and in summer. Say which way the room faces once and the rhythm follows the season.

  • What you did last time

    Tick a reminder when you water. Skip one and Sprout asks again tomorrow, then moves that plant's next date, without a lecture.

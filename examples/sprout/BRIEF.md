# Brief: Sprout

A landing page for Sprout, a watering reminder app for house plants. Friendly illustration, no photos.
What moves: a drawing of a plant grows on load: the stem draws upward and seven leaves grow out of it. Three small animated icons (a water drop, a sun, a calendar tick) are Lottie files and play only while they are on screen. Near the end, five pots fill with water as the section scrolls into view. Keep it light: no animation library for the page itself.
Words on the page, exactly: headline "Your plants, watered on the day they need it." Button "Get Sprout".
Look: sunny yellow, deep leaf ink, terracotta pots. Humanist sans type.
Done when: the plant is complete with reduced motion on and the icons hold one still frame, the pots fill without JavaScript in browsers that support scroll-driven CSS, and the page passes the site check.

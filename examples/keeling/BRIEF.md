# Brief: The Keeling Curve in five steps

Words on the page, exactly: headline "One instrument, sixty-seven years, a line that has not once gone down."
Look: a printed report: off-white, graphite, one red for the measurement. Serif text.
Stack: SVG, with D3 only for scales and the line shape.
Done when: the readout says 427.35 ppm at 2025, the decade figures are computed from the data, the full table and the sources are on the page, and the five states switch at once with reduced motion on.

## home
**Header.** Larkspur · Pricing · Try free
**Headline.** One inbox. One owner for every message. [C1][C3]
**Subline.** Larkspur is a shared inbox for teams of two to ten people. Mail to your team address lands in one place, and every message belongs to one person until it is answered. [C1][C2][C3]
**Actions.** Start free trial · See pricing
**Fine print.** 14 days free, no card needed. Larkspur is a made-up product, used to demonstrate brief2motion: nothing on this site is real. [C9][C10]
**Illustration caption.** Sample data: an invented message arrives and a teammate claims it. [C11]

**How a message moves through Larkspur**
1. It arrives. Mail sent to your team address lands in the shared inbox. Nobody has to forward anything. [C2]
2. Someone claims it. One person takes the message, and its owner shows on the row, so nobody starts a second reply. [C3]
3. The team sees who has it. The inbox lists every message with its owner, so "who answered that?" has an answer. [C3]

**What else is in it**
- Internal notes. Leave a note on a message for your team. Notes stay inside the team and are never sent to the sender. [C4]
- Saved replies. Keep the answers you give often, and insert one into a reply. [C5]

**Two plans.** Team: US$12 per person a month, up to 5 inboxes, 30 days of history. Studio: US$24 per person a month, unlimited inboxes, full history. [C6][C7] Link: Compare the plans.

**Questions**
- Do I need a card to try Larkspur? No. The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. [C9]
- Can I cancel? Yes, at any time. Plans are billed monthly. [C8]
- How big a team is it for? Teams of two to ten people. [C1]
- Is Larkspur a real product? No. It is made up, to demonstrate brief2motion's site mode. The prices, plans and features are invented, no email is sent and nothing you type is stored. [C10]

**Closing.** Try Larkspur free for 14 days. Start free trial.
**Footer.** Larkspur is a made-up product for a brief2motion example. Names, prices and features are invented for the demonstration. No email is sent and nothing you enter is stored.

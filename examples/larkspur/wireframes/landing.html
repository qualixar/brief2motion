How a message moves through Larkspur

It arrives

Mail sent to your team address lands in the shared inbox. Nobody has to forward anything.

Someone claims it

One person takes the message, and its owner shows on the row, so nobody starts a second reply.

The team sees who has it

The inbox lists every message with its owner, so "who answered that?" has an answer.

What else is in it

Internal notes

Leave a note on a message for your team. Notes stay inside the team and are never sent to the sender.

Saved replies

Keep the answers you give often, and insert one into a reply.

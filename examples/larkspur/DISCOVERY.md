# Discovery: Larkspur

Larkspur is a made-up product used to demonstrate site mode. The visitors below are imagined and nothing here is research; each line is an assumption taken from the brief.

## Visitors
- A team lead or founder of a small team who handles shared mail in a personal inbox or a group alias and is losing track of who answered what. They arrive from a link, usually on a phone, and decide in under a minute whether to read on.
- A teammate asked "can you try this?". They want the plans and the sign-up, not the pitch.

## Tasks
| ID | Visitor | Task | Route | Action |
|----|---------|------|-------|--------|
| T1 | Team lead | Understand what Larkspur does and who it is for | home | start-trial |
| T2 | Team lead | Compare the Team and Studio plans | pricing | start-team |
| T3 | Team lead | Start the free trial with a work email | start | submit-trial |

## Owner goal
Trial sign-ups. Success would be observed as completed trial forms per visit; there is no baseline (new product) and, in this demo, no analytics and no back end, so the measurement is PENDING.

## Constraints
- Fictional product: it must be obvious on first view and in the footer, and nothing invented may look real.
- No tracking, no cookies, no third-party requests except the pinned library CDN if one is needed; system fonts only.
- English only. Phone first. Works with motion off and with JavaScript off (the form falls back to a plain page).
- The form is a fixture: it sends nothing and stores nothing, and says so.

## Assets and claims
- No photographs, logos or people. One share image (1200 x 630) drawn for the site; everything else is HTML and CSS.
- Every fact on the pages has a row in CONTENT.md with its source. Sample data in the hero illustration is DRAFTED and labelled "Sample data".

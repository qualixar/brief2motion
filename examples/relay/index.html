The 3 a.m. page goes to one person. The right one.

Relay holds your on-call rota and sends each alert to whoever is on it right now. If they do not answer in five minutes, it moves to the next name. Nobody else's phone makes a sound.

One page, start to finish

  1. An alert arrives

    Your monitoring tool posts to Relay. Relay looks at the rota and picks the one person who is on call at that minute.

    03:07:12  alert   checkout-api latency above 2 s
03:07:12  rota    on call: Tomás (until 08:00)
03:07:13  page    Tomás · push + phone call

  2. They acknowledge, or it moves on

    One tap says "I have it" and the page stops. If five minutes pass with no tap, Relay pages the next name and tells the first one it did.

    03:07:41  ack     Tomás · "looking"
03:07:41  hold    escalation timer stopped
03:07:42  notify  #incidents · Tomás has it

  3. They resolve it, and the note stays

    When the alert clears, Relay closes the page and files what happened, with times, so the morning handover is a link and not a memory.

    03:31:05  clear   checkout-api latency normal
03:31:06  resolve Tomás · "bad deploy, rolled back"
03:31:06  file    handover note saved

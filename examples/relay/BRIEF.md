# Brief: Relay

A landing page for Relay, an on-call paging tool for small teams. No product screenshots: the headline has to do the work.
What moves: the headline sets itself word by word on load, and "one person." gets a highlight that wipes in. Under it, a small diagram shows an alert travelling to the one person on call. Further down, three rows of huge words (Page. Acknowledge. Resolve.) slide sideways as I scroll. A rota widget lets me pick a day and the highlight springs to whoever is on call.
Words on the page, exactly: headline "The 3 a.m. page goes to one person. The right one." Button "Start a rota".
Look: fog grey-blue paper, night ink, signal amber only where a page is live. Big heavy sans type.
Stack: plain HTML, no build step.
Done when: the headline reads as one sentence to a screen reader, nothing moves with reduced motion on, the rota works from the keyboard, and the page passes the site check on desktop and phone.

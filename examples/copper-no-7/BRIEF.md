# Brief: Copper No. 7

What moves: my perfume bottle forms from dust when the page loads. As I scroll, three ingredients (cardamom, rose, amber) form from the same dust one after another, and then it is the bottle again. A paper plane flies across the hero while I scroll.
Words on the page, exactly: headline "Bottled from a single copper still." Button "Reserve a bottle".
Images: a product photo on a white background and three ingredient photos.
Look: light, paper and ink, one burnt-orange accent.
Done when: the bottle is recognisable at the end of the scroll, p95 frame time is 20 ms or less on desktop and 40 ms on the emulated phone, the page weighs under 1.5 MiB, and it still reads without WebGL and with reduced motion.

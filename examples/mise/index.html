How it moves, and why

  • The tab marker slides

    One marker travels between tabs, so your eye is carried to the tab you picked. It is a shared layout element, not three separate highlights.

  • Rows make room

    Drag a dinner and the others step aside as you pass. Open one and the rows below slide down together. Nothing jumps, so you never lose your place.

  • Things go somewhere

    Tick an ingredient and it travels to the bought list. Remove a dinner and it shrinks away while the rest close the gap.

  • Numbers settle

    The count of things left to buy rolls to its new value on a spring. A spring can be interrupted, so fast ticking never queues up.

Every one of these answers something you did. Nothing on this page moves on its own, and with reduced motion switched on the same changes happen at once.

# Brief: Tidewater Salt Works

A brand site that tells the story of how we make sea salt, as you scroll.
What moves: the headline rises line by line on load and a tide line draws across the hero. A one-sentence paragraph lights up word by word as it crosses the screen. Then a pinned scene: a picture sequence of a salt crystal growing, scrubbed by the scroll, with three captions (Flood, Wait, Rake). Then the year in six cards that travel sideways while the section is pinned (a normal swipe row on phones). Then a tide chart that draws itself.
Words on the page, exactly: headline "Salt, made by the tide."
Images: none supplied. Draw the crystal sequence in code.
Look: deep slate water, salt white, one rust accent, serif headlines.
Stack: GSAP with ScrollTrigger, smooth scrolling.
Done when: the crystal picture changes with the scroll position, nothing is pinned or scrubbed with reduced motion on, the page works with JavaScript off, and it weighs under 1.5 MiB with all 48 frames.

# Kinetic typography

Family: Motion-led approaches.

The words are the animation: letters, words and lines move, stretch and take turns, so reading and watching become one thing.

## Signature rules

- Split text into lines, words or characters and stagger them
- One typeface, very large
- Movement follows the reading order
- Keep the real sentence available to screen readers

## Worked example

`examples/relay/` is a complete site built this way, with its brief, its motion plan at the top of its script and a passing `site-report.json`. The stack reference to read is `references/stacks/motion.md`.

## Motion that fits

A set headline on load (0.6 to 1.2 s), then words that react to scroll. Stagger 30 to 60 ms per word.

## Accessibility

- Split text must stay one sentence for assistive technology
- Text that never stops moving needs a pause, or must end within 5 seconds (WCAG 2.2.2)

## Choose it for

- SaaS and product launches with a strong line and no picture, agencies, manifestos

## Avoid it for

- Pages where the message is in a picture or a demo
